Clinic UPS Battery Bank Replacement in Jafferkhanpet

Case Study: Overhaul of a 64-cell Amaron Quanta SMF battery bank, terminal torqueing, battery rack cleaning, and AMC onboarding for a medical clinic in Jafferkhanpet.

Healthcare Power Backup

Project Overview

A multi-specialty healthcare clinic in Jafferkhanpet, Chennai, reported that their 30 KVA online UPS system was failing to hold battery backup during grid trips, threatening diagnostic equipment and electronic medical record servers.

Diagnostic & Action Taken

Our technicians conducted a digital conductance and internal resistance audit on the existing battery string. Four degraded cells had dragged down the entire string voltage.

  • Battery Replacement: Supplied 64 fresh 12V 100Ah Amaron Quanta VRLA SMF batteries
  • Installation: Complete busbar cleaning, insulated copper cable crimping, and terminal anti-corrosion greasing
  • Recycling: Safe pickup and authorized environmental recycling of the old battery bank
  • AMC Agreement: Onboarded into a comprehensive AMC plan with quarterly health reports
