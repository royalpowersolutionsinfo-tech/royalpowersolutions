Hybrid Solar & UPS Power System in West Mambalam

Case Study: 5kWp rooftop solar PV installation, hybrid LiFePO4 battery storage, zero-transfer UPS integration, and TANGEDCO net metering for a residential home office.

Residential Hybrid Solar

Project Overview & Requirements

A residential client operating a remote consulting firm in West Mambalam, Chennai, needed to drastically reduce high summer electricity bills while ensuring 100% uninterrupted power for computer workstations and air conditioning during grid power cuts.

System Design & Setup

Royal Power Solutions designed an integrated hybrid solar PV system backed by lithium-ion battery storage.

  • Solar PV Array: 5kWp Mono-PERC high-efficiency solar panels on elevated terrace mounting structures
  • Hybrid Inverter: 5KVA Hybrid MPPT Solar Inverter with built-in UPS transfer switch
  • Storage Bank: 48V 100Ah LiFePO4 Lithium-Ion Battery pack (compact wall-mount)
  • Net Metering: TANGEDCO bidirectional solar net-meter application and grid synchronization approval
