500 KVA Industrial UPS Installation in Ambattur

Case Study: Turnkey engineering, electrical panel wiring, and 3-phase power continuity implementation for a precision automotive component plant in Ambattur SIDCO.

Manufacturing Plant Project

Project Overview & Client Requirement

An automotive component manufacturing plant located in Ambattur Industrial Estate, Chennai, experienced frequent micro-outages and voltage transients on the utility line. These power drops resulted in automatic shutoffs on 12 multi-axis CNC machining centers, leading to tool breakage, corrupted part dimensions, and unscheduled production line downtime.

Technical Scope & Solution

Royal Power Solutions was contracted to design, supply, install, and commission a complete 3-phase industrial power backup system capable of handling high motor inrush currents and non-linear harmonic loads.

  • Equipment Supplied: 3-Phase 500 KVA Online Double-Conversion Industrial UPS (IGBT Inverter Topology)
  • Battery Bank: 2V VRLA Heavy-Duty Cell Bank (120 Cells, rack-mounted) delivering 30 minutes full load backup
  • Isolation Transformer: 500 KVA K-13 rated Faraday shielded isolation transformer to eliminate neutral voltage drift
  • Switchgear: 4-Pole Motorized Bypass Switch & Form-3B LT distribution panel

Execution Process

Step-by-Step Engineering Execution

1. Load Profiling & Harmonic Scan

Engineers conducted 72-hour power quality logging, analyzing peak inrush current during simultaneous CNC motor startup and measuring total harmonic distortion (THD).

2. Logistics & Rigging

Safe unloading and shifting of the 3.8-ton UPS cabinets and heavy battery racks using specialized hydraulic equipment without disturbing factory operations.

3. Cable Laying & Earthing

Laying copper armored cables in heavy-duty cable trays, installing chemical earth pits (< 1 Ohm neutral-to-earth resistance), and interconnecting busbars.

4. Commissioning & Load Testing

Step-load testing using dummy load banks up to 100% capacity, verifying zero-millisecond transfer on main breaker trip, and issuing commissioning certificates.
