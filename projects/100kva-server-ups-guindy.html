100 KVA IT Server Room UPS & Stabilizer Setup in Guindy

Case Study: High-availability power conditioning, N+1 parallel redundant 100 KVA online UPS, 50 KVA servo stabilizer, and SNMP remote monitoring integration in Guindy Corporate Hub.

Corporate IT Facility

Project Overview & Requirements

A growing software development and cloud hosting facility in Guindy, Chennai, required a zero-downtime power protection upgrade for their expanded server room housing over 80 active blade racks, core network switches, and precision cooling units.

Solution Implemented

Royal Power Solutions designed and installed a dual-path power architecture ensuring zero millisecond transfer time and total voltage stability.

  • UPS System: 100 KVA 3-Phase Online Double-Conversion UPS with parallel N+1 card
  • Voltage Regulation: 50 KVA Servo Voltage Stabilizer maintaining output at 415V ±1%
  • Battery Bank: 32 x 12V 150Ah High-Rate Discharge SMF batteries in a modular rack cabinet
  • Monitoring: SNMP Network Management Card integrated into the client's IT monitoring dashboard for temperature and battery health alerts
