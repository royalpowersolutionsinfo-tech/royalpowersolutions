Isolation Transformers for Hospitals, IT & Industry in Chennai

An isolation transformer electrically separates the primary (supply) and secondary (load) circuits, eliminating earth loops, reducing electrical noise and protecting sensitive equipment from surges and transients. Royal Power Solutions supplies and installs Faraday-shielded isolation transformers for hospitals (medical IT networks), data centres, CNC machines, laboratories and industrial equipment across Chennai.

Why Use an Isolation Transformer?

Hospitals: Creates safe IT (Isolated Terra) electrical networks protecting patients from leakage current.

IT Rooms: Eliminates earth loops and common-mode noise that cause data errors and equipment failures.

Laboratories: Provides clean, interference-free power for sensitive measurement instruments.

Industry: Protects CNC machines, PLCs and automation equipment from supply-side faults.
