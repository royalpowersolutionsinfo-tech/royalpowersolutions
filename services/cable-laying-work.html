DC Inter-Cell Cable Laying

Battery-to-UPS DC Cable Laying

Main DC bus cables from battery bank to UPS DC input — cable sized for full charge/discharge current, routed through conduit or cable tray, lugged and terminated at both ends.

AC Power Cable Laying

Earthing Cable Laying

Conduit & Cable Tray Laying

Lug Crimping & Termination

Our Process

How We Do Cable Laying Work

01

Cable Sizing Calculation

Calculate required cable cross-section based on load current, distance, permissible voltage drop, and ambient temperature. Select correct cable type (XLPE, PVC, FR, FRLS).

02

Route Planning & Conduit / Tray Laying

Plan cable route to minimise length, avoid heat sources, and maintain safe segregation of DC, AC, and signal cables. Lay conduit or cable tray with correct supports and earthing.

03

Cable Pulling & Laying

Pull cables through conduit or lay in tray without exceeding minimum bend radius. Label both ends of each cable as per drawing. Secure with cable ties at regular intervals.

04

Lug Crimping & Termination

Cut cable squarely, strip insulation to correct length, crimp lugs hydraulically, apply heat shrink, and terminate at both ends with correct torque. Polarity clearly marked.

05

Testing & Verification

Continuity test, insulation resistance (IR) test, polarity check, and voltage drop measurement. All results recorded and verified before energisation.
