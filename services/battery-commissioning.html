Commissioning Process

Battery Commissioning — Step by Step

Our standardised battery commissioning procedure ensures maximum performance and full warranty compliance.

01

Site Survey & Battery Inspection

Physical inspection of all battery units — check for damage, confirm battery type, voltage rating, and verify quantity against delivery note.

02

Battery Positioning & Rack Setup

Position batteries on racks or trays, ensure proper spacing for ventilation, and arrange in correct string configuration as per UPS DC bus voltage requirements.

03

DC Cable Laying & Inter-Cell Connection

Complete DC Cable Laying — inter-cell connectors, string cables, main DC bus cables. Correct polarity, lug crimping, and termination at both battery end and UPS DC bus.

04

Initial Charge & Equalisation

Connect to UPS charger, monitor initial charging current and voltage. Perform equalisation charge if required. Identify and mark pilot cell for ongoing monitoring.

05

Load Discharge Test & Report

Perform timed load discharge test to verify battery capacity. Document all readings and produce commissioning report for warranty and maintenance records.
