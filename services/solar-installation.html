Solar System Types

On-Grid Solar

Connected to TANGEDCO grid. Export excess power. Net metering credits. No battery needed. Best ROI.

Off-Grid Solar

Fully independent. Battery storage. Best for areas with unreliable grid supply.

Hybrid Solar

Grid-connected with battery backup. Best of both worlds — export to grid and power outage protection.

Solar Installation Questions

A 3kW on-grid residential solar system in Chennai costs approximately ₹1.5 to ₹2.2 lakhs before subsidy. After the Tamil Nadu government subsidy, the net cost reduces significantly for residential installations. We provide detailed quotes after a free site survey.

Yes. We assist with the complete TANGEDCO net metering application. Once approved, excess solar energy exported to the grid is credited to your electricity bill, effectively reducing it to near zero for well-sized systems.

We install Tier-1 solar panels from leading manufacturers with 25-year performance warranties. Panel selection is based on your budget, roof type and orientation — we will recommend the best option for your situation.
