Commissioning Process

UPS Commissioning — Step by Step

Our standardised UPS commissioning procedure activates the manufacturer's warranty and ensures optimal system performance.

01

UPS Positioning

Safe positioning of the UPS in the designated location. Levelling, anti-vibration pads, and adequate clearance for ventilation and maintenance access confirmed.

02

Earthing & Bonding

Verify earth continuity, connect UPS chassis earth, neutral bonding as per system design. Earth loop impedance check performed.

03

DC Cable Laying & Battery Connection

DC Cable Laying from battery bank to UPS DC bus — correct cable sizing, lug crimping, polarity verification. Battery fuse/MCCB installed and verified.

04

Parameter Configuration

All UPS parameters configured — input/output voltage thresholds, battery type, number of cells, battery charge current, output frequency, transfer time, alarms and communication settings.

05

Load Transfer & Bypass Test

Load connected and transfer test performed. Static bypass operation verified. Mains failure simulation — confirm transfer to battery and back. Full load test if required.

06

Commissioning Report & Handover

Detailed commissioning report produced with all parameter settings, test results, serial numbers and engineer sign-off. Formal handover to client with operational briefing.
