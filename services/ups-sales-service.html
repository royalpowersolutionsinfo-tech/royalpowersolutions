UPS Technology

Understanding UPS Types — Which Do You Need?

Online (Double-Conversion) UPS

Best for: Servers, data centres, hospitals, industrial equipment.
The load always runs through the inverter. Zero transfer time. Perfect power quality. Protects against all power problems including surges, sags, spikes, noise and outages.

Line-Interactive UPS

Best for: Workstations, network equipment, small offices.
Built-in AVR (Automatic Voltage Regulation) handles minor voltage fluctuations without switching to battery. Switches to battery only for outages. 4–8ms transfer time.

Offline (Standby) UPS

Best for: Home computers, basic equipment.
Passes mains power directly to the load and only switches to battery on outage. Lowest cost but offers least protection. Not recommended for critical equipment.

3-Phase Industrial UPS

Best for: Manufacturing, large data centres, CNC machines.
High-capacity 3-phase UPS systems from 10KVA to 1000KVA for industrial and enterprise applications requiring high power and redundancy.
